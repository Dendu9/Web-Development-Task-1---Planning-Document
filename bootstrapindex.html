911 GT3

The Porsche 911 GT3 is a high-performance sports car designed for track-focused performance, featuring a 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six engine that produces 502 horsepower and revs to 9,000 rpm. It offers a 7-speed PDK or 6-speed manual transmission, with a starting MSRP of around $230,500. Key features include a swan-neck rear wing, extensive carbon fiber (CFRP) usage, and advanced aerodynamics.
